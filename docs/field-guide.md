# MCA Steward App — Field Guide

**For clan stewards and field officers · Version 3 · October 2026**

This guide covers using the app in the field. For setup see the
[Installation Guide](installation-guide.md); for coordinator tasks see the
[Administrator's Manual](administrators-manual.md).

---

## 1. Logging in

The app uses your **fingerprint** to identify you — no username or password.

- Open the app from the **home-screen icon** (not a browser tab).
- Tap to log in and use your enrolled finger. If it fails, press firmly and
  hold; after three tries wait a moment and try again.
- If you are completely locked out, your coordinator must re-enrol you.

The app works **offline**. You can record everything with no signal; it saves
to the phone and uploads later when you have internet.

---

## 2. How monitoring works — register first, then monitor

Most things you monitor go through two stages:

1. **Register it once.** Walk to the water source, facility, instrument or
   tree, and record its type, location (GPS) and a reference photo. It is then
   **Pending Approval**.
2. **Monitor it monthly.** After your coordinator approves it and assigns it to
   **your clan**, it appears as a task. You take the monthly reading.

**You only see your own clan's approved items.** If something belongs to
another clan, it will not appear on your phone — that is deliberate, so two
clans don't both monitor (and get paid for) the same thing. Each item can be
read **once per clan per month**; after you record it, it shows **"Done this
month."**

Every registration and every monthly reading needs a **photo**.

## 3. Water Quality

Open **Water Quality** from the dashboard.

- **🗂 Manage Water Sources** → register a new source (name, type, GPS, photo,
  description). Types are grouped:
  - **Natural:** River, Creek, Spring
  - **Engineered / manmade:** Reservoir & pipes, Ram pump, Solar pump,
    Well / borehole
- Your clan's **approved** sources appear as monitoring tasks. Tap a *Due* one,
  walk to it (the app unlocks within 30 m and asks for a fingerprint), then
  **Begin Reading**.

What you record depends on the type:

- **River:** water observations (colour, odour, flow, pollution) **plus a
  Secchi disc** depth reading.
- **Creek:** water observations **plus pond skaters** (surface insects — more
  usually means healthier water) **and shading** over the water.
- **Spring:** the core water observations.
- **Engineered (reservoir / pump / well):** a two-part check —
  - **Source condition:** is water present/flowing, is it clean & clear, any
    contamination signs.
  - **Delivery & infrastructure:** water at the tap/outlet, leaks, damage to
    pipes/tank/pump, and any repair needed.

Always finish with a **photo** of the source today.

## 4. Service Delivery Monitoring (schools & health)

Open **Service Delivery Monitoring**.

- **🗂 Manage Facilities** → register a school, health centre or aid post
  (name, type, GPS, photo, description) → Pending Approval.
- Your clan's approved facilities appear as tasks. Tap one to record a visit.

The visit form records whether the facility was **open / partial / closed**,
**staff present**, and:

- **Schools:** total **students present** today.
- **Health centres / aid posts:** whether **essential medicines** are in stock.

Add notes and a **photo** of the facility today, then submit.

---

## 5. Weather Station / Met instruments

Open **Weather Station**.

- **🗂 Manage Met Stations** → register an instrument (name/location, type,
  GPS, photo). Types: **Rain gauge, Weather station, Water level meter**.
- Your clan's approved instruments appear as tasks. Tap one to record.

The reading form is centred on **equipment function**:

- Is the instrument **working / faulty / not working**? Any **damage**
  (none / minor / major)?
- **Rain gauge:** rainfall since last reading (mm), and whether you emptied the
  gauge afterwards.
- **Water level meter:** level at the marker (cm) and flow condition.
- **Weather station:** both of the above.

Add notes and a **photo**, then submit.

---

## 6. Phenology (monitored trees)

Open **Phenology Monitoring**.

- **Manage Plots** → establish a plant (crop, GPS, photo) → Pending Approval.
- Once approved and assigned to your clan, approved plants appear under
  **Begin Monthly Observations**.
- For each plant record its **phenophase** (vegetative / flowering / fruiting /
  ripe / dormant) and **health** (good / fair / poor).

As with everything else, you only see **your clan's** approved trees, so two
clans don't both claim the same tree.

---

## 7. Sending your data

Open **Send Data**.

- **Send All New Records** uploads everything not yet sent (needs signal).
- If there's no direct signal, the app can open the Android **share sheet** so
  you can send the backup file to your coordinator via WhatsApp, or transfer it
  by USB. Your coordinator uploads it to Drive.
- **Storage Health** should show **"Data is protected."** If it says *NOT
  protected*, the app was opened from a browser tab — reinstall it from the
  home-screen icon (ask your coordinator).

> **Your data is safe while the phone has space and the app is installed to the
> home screen.** Send your records whenever you find signal so nothing is lost
> if the phone is damaged.

## 8. Quick reference

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Log in | Fingerprint on the opening screen |
| Register a new thing | *Manage …* button inside its module |
| Do a monthly reading | Tap a *Due* item in the module's task list |
| See only my clan's work | Automatic — others' items are hidden |
| Send data | **Send Data → Send All New Records** |
| Check data is protected | **Send Data → Storage Health** |
