Livelihood Site Visits

Standard report of field site-visit records for livelihood project sites.
About this report. One entry per recorded site visit. Each visit captures the project's structure & status, observed signs of use and condition/survival, any field notes, issues arising, and inputs needed — plus photos and a GPS location. Location is captured automatically; where a fix wasn't available the record is still kept and flagged for review. Photos are held on Drive and linked by filename.
