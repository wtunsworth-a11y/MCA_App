# MCA Steward App — Visiting Consultant Guide

**For visiting consultants · Version 1 · October 2026**

A short guide for consultants who visit the Managalas and need to record the
**training they deliver** and **map the sites they visit**. For installing the
app see the [Installation Guide](installation-guide.md).

---

## 1. What you can do

As a **Visiting Consultant** your app is kept simple — your dashboard shows just
three things:

- **Training Records** — log any training or workshop you deliver.
- **Field Mapping** — mark the sites you visit, for your own record.
- **Export Data** — send your records to the project when you have internet.

You don't need a clan or a zone, and you won't see the clan monitoring tools —
those are for the local stewards.

The app works **offline**. Record everything with no signal; it saves on the
phone and uploads later.

---

## 2. Getting set up (once, with a coordinator)

A project coordinator sets you up on a phone:

1. They enter the coordinator passcode and choose the role **Visiting
   Consultant**.
2. They enter your **name** and a short **ID** for you.
3. You register your **fingerprint** — that's how you log in (no password).

Then open the app from the **home-screen icon** and tap to log in with your
finger.

> **If a "Check your phone's date" warning appears**, your phone's clock is
> wrong and every record would get the wrong date. Fix it first: phone
> **Settings → Date & Time → Automatic / Network-provided**, then tap
> **"I've fixed it — re-check"**. All dates and times in the app are **PNG local
> time**.

---

## 3. Recording training you deliver

Open **Training Records → add a session** and fill in:

- **Date** (defaults to today) and **type / topic** of the training.
- **Who attended** — number of **women, men and youth**.
- Any **materials** handed out, **outcomes**, follow-up needed, and challenges.
- A **photo** of the session if you can.

Tap **Save**. The session is stored on the phone and will upload when you sync.

---

## 4. Mapping the sites you visit

Open **Field Mapping** to keep your own record of where you went:

1. Tap to start a new map and choose what it is:
   - a **point** (a single place — a village, a nursery, a meeting site), or
   - a **line** or **area** if you want to trace a track or a block.
2. Give it a **name** and a short **note**.
3. **Walk to the spot** (or around the area) and let it take the **GPS** — a live
   preview shows the point or shape as it records.
4. Add a **photo** if useful, then **Save**.

You can reopen **Field Mapping** any time to see the sites you've already marked.

> Most sites are small, so there's no background map yet — the preview shows your
> point or shape on its own. That's enough to record where you were.

---

## 5. Sending your records to the project

When you have internet (WiFi or mobile data):

- Open **Export Data** (or tap the **"not yet sent — tap to send"** banner on the
  dashboard) and send.
- It uploads your training records and maps. If you sync more than once, nothing
  is duplicated — each record is sent only once.

Do this before you hand the phone back or leave the field, so nothing is lost.

---

## 6. If something goes wrong

- **A photo or video can't be repeated** (people have moved on) — don't worry,
  the app never throws your work away. If GPS can't get a fix (you're indoors, or
  under trees), you can **retry location** or save without it; your photos stay.
- **The app stops you and a warning won't clear** — tap **Report a Problem** (on
  your Profile) so it's logged with a screenshot, and tell your coordinator.
- **Locked out of login** — your coordinator can re-enrol you.
